# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Submitting spam, malicious links, or deliberately false offer information

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an Issue or contacting the maintainers directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions) and when representing the project in public spaces.
